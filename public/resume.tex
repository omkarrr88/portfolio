\begin{rSection}{PROJECTS}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{VayuNetra -- AI-Powered Urban Air Quality Intelligence (August 2026):} A LangGraph multi-agent platform that turns air quality readings into attributed, delivered interventions across 10 Indian cities (top 10 of 15,000+ teams, ET AI Hackathon 2.0): per-km\textsuperscript{2} PM2.5 source attribution (GBM + SHAP), 72-hour calibrated forecasts, RAG-cited enforcement dossiers with draft notice PDFs, and citizen advisories in 8 languages over app, Telegram, and IVR -- 16,529 modeled cells on zero-cost infrastructure. \href{https://github.com/omkarrr88/VayuNetra}{\textit{GitHub}}

\item \textbf{Chakravyuh -- Multi-Agent RL for UPI Fraud Detection (April 2026):} A five-agent OpenEnv RL environment (scammer, victim, analyzer, bank monitor, regulator) for UPI fraud (7th of 31,000+ teams, Meta PyTorch Hackathon). A Qwen2.5-7B analyzer fine-tuned with LoRA and GRPO reaches 99.3\% detection at 6.7\% false positives (36\% before a reward-hacking fix), statistically tied with Llama-3.3-70B at 10$\times$ fewer parameters. Built the evaluation suite (frontier baselines, significance tests, leakage audit) and the Gradio demo. \href{https://github.com/omkarrr88/Chakravyuh}{\textit{GitHub}}

\item \textbf{Fitmon -- Camera-Refereed Fitness Game (September 2026):} An Android fitness game where the front camera grades every rep (top 7 of 7,000+ teams, iQOO Pune City Battle). On-device MediaPipe pose tracking scores depth, range, tempo, and alignment, turned into damage against an adaptive boss; an on-device Gemma 3n coach cites only measured data. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, CameraX, Room, Firebase; 836 JVM tests. \href{https://github.com/omkarrr88/ClashFit}{\textit{GitHub}}

\item \textbf{PyTorch Training Run Debugger (April 2026):} An OpenEnv RL environment (Meta PyTorch Hackathon, Round 1) where AI agents debug broken training runs: 7 real failures (exploding/vanishing gradients, data leakage, overfitting, BatchNorm in eval mode, training-loop bugs, bad LR schedules), with rewards gated on what the agent has inspected. FastAPI, WebSockets, Hugging Face Spaces; 245 tests, 95\% coverage. \href{https://github.com/omkarrr88/PyTorch-Training-Run-Debugger}{\textit{GitHub}}

\item \textbf{V2V Communication for Blind Spot Detection and Accident Prevention (May 2026):} A simulation where vehicles warn each other about blind spots and likely collisions before they happen. Built the messaging between vehicles, the sensor data processing, and a risk score that decides when to alert the driver. The work became a research paper, now under review at \textit{Discover Internet of Things} (Springer Nature). \href{https://github.com/omkarrr88/V2V}{\textit{GitHub}}

\item \textbf{Smart PUC (May 2026):} A blockchain-based system for monitoring vehicle emissions and PUC compliance in India. OBD devices sign live emission telemetry, testing stations validate it, and the records go on-chain so no single party can fake the data. Tracks all five Bharat Stage VI pollutants with physics-based models and an ML ensemble for fraud detection, and issues emission certificates as NFTs. Built with Solidity smart contracts (Hardhat, OpenZeppelin), a FastAPI/Web3.py backend, and a web dashboard. \href{https://github.com/omkarrr88/Smart_PUC}{\textit{GitHub}}
